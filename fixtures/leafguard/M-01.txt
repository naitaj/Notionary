Meeting: LeafGuard Edge Deployment Kickoff & Constraints (M-01)
Date: 2026-02-05
Attendees: Karan Mehta, Ananya Patel, Vikram

Karan Mehta: We are developing LeafGuard for resource-constrained edge devices in rural Madhya Pradesh where internet connectivity is non-existent.
